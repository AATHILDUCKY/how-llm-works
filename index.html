Quick reference · Core LLM concepts

Tokens & embeddings

Text becomes tokens, token IDs and numeric vectors. Position information helps the model track word order.

Transformers & attention

Transformer layers use causal self-attention and neural network operations to process relationships across tokens.

Generating text

The model produces a probability distribution for the next token, chooses a token and repeats.

Pretraining

The model learns statistical patterns by predicting text and updating its parameters to reduce error.

Instruction tuning

Additional tuning and preference-based training can make outputs more helpful, safe and instruction-following.

RAG and limitations

Retrieval can bring external information into context. Models can still hallucinate; important claims need checking.
